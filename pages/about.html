Our Team

Get to know the talented individuals behind our success.

We are a group of ten IFT 211 (Introduction to Web Technologies) students, Level 200, from Bayero University Kano originally from Kaduna State. For this assignment we have collaborated to design and build a website that showcases the history, culture, landmarks, cuisine, festivals, and everyday life of our hometown. This project highlights our skills in HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript while preserving and sharing the community stories that matter most to us. We aim to give visitors an authentic, engaging look at the people, places, and traditions that make our region unique.
